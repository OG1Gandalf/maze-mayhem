How to play
  • Controls are relative to your character: Up = step forward, Left/Right = turn 90° and step, Down = turn around and step. Into a wall you only turn.
  • Desktop: arrow keys (or WASD), Space shoots, M enlarges the map.
  • Mobile: D-pad bottom-left, Fire bottom-right, tap the map to enlarge it.
  • Reach the golden light pillar in the centre first. 10 HP, shots cost 1 HP.
  • Corrupt NPCs hunt you down: bribe them with cash, kill them or walk away. Never fail a bribe with the police!
