How to play
  • Desktop: arrow keys move one cell, Space shoots.
  • Mobile: Up/Down buttons, tap left/right half of the screen to turn and move, Fire button to shoot.
  • Reach the glowing centre first. 10 HP, shots cost 1 HP.
  • Corrupt NPCs block your route: negotiate, kill or walk away. Never fail a bribe with the police!
